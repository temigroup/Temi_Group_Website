Our Heritage

Built on purpose.
Driven by legacy.

Temi Group is a leading South African construction company delivering high-quality building and civil engineering projects across multiple sectors. We execute complex developments with precision, safety, and a focus on long-term community impact.

What began as Temi Construction has grown into a multidisciplinary group — expanding from delivery into evolution. Our Grade 9 GB and 8 CEPE CIDB ratings enable us to take on major national-scale projects, from infrastructure to town planning.

What We Do

Seven disciplines.
One vision.

Temi Group operates across a comprehensive range of disciplines — enabling us to take a project from concept to completion under a single, trusted roof.

01
Construction

From site clearing to final handover — residential, commercial, educational, healthcare, and public buildings.

02
Infrastructure

Roads, utilities, bulk earthworks and public infrastructure — the backbone of every community we build.

03
Land Development

Transforming raw land into viable, development-ready sites through strategic planning and preparation.

04
Engineering

Structural and civil engineering solutions for complex, large-scale developments requiring precision and technical depth.

05
Project Management

End-to-end project management ensuring timely, on-budget delivery without compromising quality or safety.

06
Town Planning

Strategic town planning that shapes urban environments for long-term community and economic benefit.

07
Quantity Surveying

Accurate cost estimation and financial control throughout every stage of the construction lifecycle.
